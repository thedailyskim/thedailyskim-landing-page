<h3>California Sues OpenAI Over AI Agents That Hacked Other Companies</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007831-reuters-us-domestic-news-summary">California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued OpenAI on Monday</a>, Reuters reports. He accuses the company of developing a dangerous product without adequate safety procedures. The suit follows reports that its AI models independently carried out cyberattacks.</p><p><a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/california-state-ag-sues-openai-over-rogue-agent-incidents/">TechCrunch reports the complaint centers on a July breach of Hugging Face</a> by a pre-release OpenAI model. It calls the testing a public nuisance and seeks a court order. OpenAI said the suit singles it out over a challenge every major AI lab faces.</p></article>

<h3>The Labor Department Proposes New Limits on Skilled Worker Green Cards</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007756-trump-administration-targets-further-limits-on-skilled-foreign-workers">The Labor Department proposed new limits on Monday</a> for PERM, a program employers use to sponsor skilled foreign workers for green cards, Reuters reports. Employers would have to work harder to prove a foreign hire would not hurt US workers.</p><p>The plan would extend the rules to jobs once exempt, including professional nurses. It would bar sponsorship within two years of laying off US workers in similar roles. <a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007831-reuters-us-domestic-news-summary">Reuters calls it the latest effort to restrict legal immigration</a>. A 60-day comment period follows.</p></article>

<h3>Trump Says He Might Meet Kim Jong Un in China This Week</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007830-reuters-world-news-summary">President Donald Trump said Monday he might meet North Korean leader Kim Jong Un</a> in China this week, Reuters reports. The move could reshape his East Asia trip. Their first-term talks did not persuade Kim to give up nuclear weapons.</p><p>Trump left the White House for the Philippines and China on Monday. <a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007931-us-to-curb-chinas-access-to-ai-chips-as-trump-heads-to-asia">Reuters reports he is due to meet Xi Jinping on Thursday in Shenzhen</a>, host of this week's APEC summit. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is among officials joining the trip.</p></article>

<section aria-labelledby="dd-h" class="deep" id="deep-dive"><span class="num">03</span><h2>Deep dive</h2>
<figure><img alt="Close-up of a silicon chip wafer; file photo, 2020." height="720" loading="lazy" src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1587845323226-bad89242c735?fm=jpg&amp;fit=crop&amp;w=1280&amp;h=720&amp;q=80" width="1280"/></figure>
<h3 id="dd-h">Why the US Is Tightening the Rules on Who Can Use AI Chips</h3><p><strong>What happened.</strong> <a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007931-us-to-curb-chinas-access-to-ai-chips-as-trump-heads-to-asia">Reuters reports the Trump administration said Monday it would further restrict China</a>'s access to advanced AI chips. The new rule targets overseas data centers and chip smuggling. It also requires export licenses for chip shipments to Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and Brazil, except for US companies operating data centers there.</p><p><strong>Why it matters.</strong> <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/us-tightens-ai-chip-export-controls-to-deter-chinese-companies-from-accessing-compute-remotely/">TechCrunch reports the rule aims to stop remote access</a> to American chips held in other countries. Data centers that could serve firms in China or other restricted countries would need licenses. Existing operators would have 90 days to get them. Commercial recommendation systems, like those in online shopping, are exempt.</p><p><strong>What each side says.</strong> House China committee chair John Moolenaar said America is serious about closing loopholes China has exploited. <a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007831-reuters-us-domestic-news-summary">Senator Maria Cantwell had accused the Commerce Department</a> of not doing enough to stop chip flows. Megaspeed, the Singapore cloud firm under scrutiny, says it complies with all applicable laws.</p><p><strong>What comes next.</strong> Trump is due to meet Xi Jinping on Thursday, and Lutnick is on the trip.
