<section aria-labelledby="p1" class="part"><span class="num">01</span><h2 id="p1">The big read</h2>
<article class="story" id="s1">

<h3>A Fed Official Says Inflation Is Moving in the Wrong Direction</h3>
<p>Kansas City Fed President Jeff Schmid said Sunday the central bank has some work to do on inflation. <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/fed-has-some-work-to-do-on-inflation-kansas-city-fed-chief-says.html">CNBC reports he said price pressures are running too hot</a>, with inflation uncomfortably above the 2% target. He did not specifically call for more rate hikes.</p><p>CNBC reported Sunday that futures markets fully priced a quarter-point hike in December. <a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007139-wall-st-week-ahead-nvidia-results-anthropic-ipo-pose-tests-for-tumultuous-ai-trade">Reuters reports Tuesday's retail sales report</a> will show how shoppers are coping with higher gasoline prices and borrowing costs. Walmart, Target, Home Depot and Lowe's also report results. Before the open, <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/stock-market-today-live-updates.html">CNBC's live page showed S&amp;P 500 futures down 0.25% at about 6:07 a.m. ET Monday</a>.</p></article>
<article class="story" id="s2">
<figure><img alt="Rocket climbing through clouds; file photo, 2026." height="720" loading="lazy" src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1776376363621-dea495339d15?fm=jpg&amp;fit=crop&amp;w=1280&amp;h=720&amp;q=80" width="1280"/></figure>
<h3>A Vulcan Rocket Fails After Liftoff and Destroys 40 Amazon Satellites</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007263-reuters-science-news-summary">Reuters reports a United Launch Alliance Vulcan rocket failed</a> roughly 10 minutes after liftoff from Cape Canaveral on Sunday. It destroyed 40 satellites for Amazon's Leo internet network. It was the launch company's first mission failure.</p><p>ULA is a joint venture of Boeing and Lockheed Martin. The cause was not immediately clear, <a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007214-reuters-world-news-summary">Reuters reports</a>. The loss is a major setback for Vulcan, the company's new flagship rocket, as it sought a regular launch pace for Amazon.</p></article>

<figure><img alt="Old Town skyline of Tallinn, Estonia; file photo, 2026." height="720" loading="lazy" src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1778267765903-6d7bbb08cfaa?fm=jpg&amp;fit=crop&amp;w=1280&amp;h=720&amp;q=80" width="1280"/></figure>
<h3>Estonia Will Close Its Border Crossings With Russia After a Suspected Arson Attempt</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007214-reuters-world-news-summary">Reuters reports Estonia will close all its border crossing points with Russia</a> from Dec. 1. Authorities detained eight people over a suspected attempted arson attack on drone maker Threod Systems. Officials said Russia's GRU military intelligence ordered it.</p><p><a href="https://www.vm.ee/en/news/foreign-minister-tsahkna-estonia-close-road-border-crossings-russia">Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna said the closures send a clear message to the Kremlin</a>. He said Russia's hostile actions come at a cost. He plans to press for tougher sanctions and a stricter visa policy at Monday's EU foreign ministers meeting in Brussels.</p></article>

<h3>Huge Crowds Rally in Prague Against a Plan to Change Public Media Funding</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007214-reuters-world-news-summary">Reuters reports hundreds of thousands of Czechs rallied in Prague</a> on Sunday against a government media funding plan. It would cut a public media fee and move funding to the state budget. Critics say that would dent broadcasters' independence.</p><p><a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/thousands-rally-in-czech-republic-against-state-media-funding-changes">Al Jazeera reports the current monthly household fee is 205 crowns, about $9.45</a>. Prime Minister Andrej Babis's government says editorial independence will not be affected. The bill passed a preliminary vote and awaits further debate. The rally came days before the Nov. 17 holiday marking the Velvet Revolution.</p></article>

<section aria-labelledby="dd-h" class="deep" id="deep-dive"><span class="num">03</span><h2>Deep dive</h2>
<figure><img alt="Lab worker holding a sample plate; file photo, 2021." height="720" loading="lazy" src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1624957485560-47747511b32f?fm=jpg&amp;fit=crop&amp;w=1280&amp;h=720&amp;q=80" width="1280"/></figure>
<h3 id="dd-h">Why Congo's Ebola Outbreak Is Still Out of Control After Six Months</h3><p><strong>What happened.</strong> <a href="https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4007191-reuters-health-news-summary">Reuters reports Africa CDC says the Ebola outbreak in Congo is still out of control</a> six months after it was declared. Director-general Jean Kaseya cited deadly attacks on health workers, unpaid staff and a lack of resources. More than 11,200 cases and 5,500 deaths have been recorded since May 15.</p><p><strong>Why it matters.</strong> <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/africa-cdc-says-ebola-outbreak-in-dr-congo-still-not-under-control">Al Jazeera reports it is the second-largest and second-deadliest Ebola outbreak on record</a>. Cases have been reported in East, Central and West Africa, the widest spread on the continent yet. No vaccine or treatment exists specifically for the Bundibugyo strain.
