<h3>A Delta System Outage Delays Flights Days Before Thanksgiving</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/delta-automation-system-issue-fixed-delays-remain-ahead-thanksgiving-rcna610821">Over 1,000 Delta flights were delayed Sunday after a system that calculates aircraft weight malfunctioned</a>, the AP reports via NBC News. The FAA issued about an hourlong ground stop for some flights into Atlanta. The FAA said the delays were not tied to its systems.</p><p><a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/delta-air-lines-nationwide-delays-amid-baggage-network-system-issue/">Delta said Sunday the system was fixed</a>, CBS News reports. As of Sunday evening, Delta had canceled 202 flights. Delta said it was rebooking customers and offering refunds to those who no longer wish to travel. <a href="https://abcnews.com/US/equipment-outage-atlanta-causes-1300-delta-flight-delays/story?id=138360900">Delta also said the outage was not the result of a hack</a>, ABC News reports.</p></article>

<h3>Storms on Both Coasts Could Disrupt Thanksgiving Travel</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/rough-weather-expected-hit-both-coasts-may-disrupt-thanksgiving-travel-rcna610778">A storm could bring 50 mph gusts and coastal flooding to the Northeast on Thanksgiving</a>, forecasters said, NBC News reports. New York City warned of gusts of 40 to 45 mph. Macy's parade balloons cannot fly in gusts above 34 mph.</p><p><a href="https://abcnews.com/US/west-coast-bracing-coastal-flooding-thanksgiving-travel-week/story?id=138357189">A coastal flood warning covers San Francisco Bay shorelines from Monday morning through Thursday afternoon</a>, ABC News reports. San Francisco Mayor Daniel Lurie urged residents to consider not traveling for the holiday. Rain is expected to sweep across Northern California on Wednesday.</p></article>

<h3>Ukraine Says Its Naval Drones Sank a Russian Warship Off Libya</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/world/ukraine/ukraine-sinks-russian-warship-merkury-libya-naval-drones-war-rcna610822">Zelenskyy said Monday that seven Ukrainian naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury</a>, NBC News reports. Ukraine's Security Service said the attack happened near the Libyan port of Benghazi. That is some 1,500 miles from the front lines, it said.</p><p><a href="https://abcnews.com/International/ukrainian-naval-drones-sank-russias-merkury-warship-libya/story?id=138369738">Zelenskyy said a Russian cargo ship, the Angara, was also hit</a>, ABC News reports. He said a Russian Ka-27 helicopter was destroyed too. <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ukraine-zelenskyy-2-russian-ships-libya/">Zelenskyy said Moscow used the Angara to transport weapons</a>, CBS News reports.</p></article>

<h3>Democrats Clinch Their Largest House Majority Since 1992</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/house-democrats-win-largest-house-majority-hubbard-north-carolina-rcna610708">Democrat Chuck Hubbard defeated GOP Rep. Virginia Foxx in North Carolina, NBC News projects</a>. NBC News projects Democrats will hold at least 258 House seats. That would be the largest Democratic majority since 1992, NBC News reports.</p><p><a href="https://abcnews.com/Politics/democrat-chuck-hubbard-wins-recount-north-carolina-us/story?id=138362634">Hubbard won a recount after leading Foxx 48.7% to 48.6% with 99% of the estimated vote reported</a>, ABC News reports. <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/democrat-chuck-hubbard-defeats-gop-rep-virginia-foxx-north-carolina/">Hubbard said Foxx called him Sunday to concede</a>, CBS News reports. Foxx has represented the state in the House since 2005.</p></article>
