<h3>The United States Proposes New Tariffs on 14 Trading Partners</h3>
<p><a href="https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural">The U.S. Trade Representative said Wednesday that 14 economies use practices tied to excess manufacturing capacity</a>. The list includes China, the European Union, Japan, India, Korea and Vietnam. USTR said practices in those economies burden or restrict U.S. commerce.</p><p><a href="https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/world/china/us-proposes-new-tariffs-14-trading-partners-fight-excess-factory-capacity-2026-11-25/">Proposed maximum rates run from 20% to 40%, but USTR intends to cap them at levels agreed in trade deals</a>, Reuters reports. China would face a capped 20% rate. <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/us-tariffs-trump-section-301.html">Greer told CNBC the tariffs likely will not take effect for months</a>, and Reuters says a public hearing is set for Jan. 25.</p></article>

<figure><img alt="People with umbrellas in heavy rain; file photo." height="720" loading="lazy" src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1667981663831-c395f9505e1d?fm=jpg&amp;fit=crop&amp;w=1280&amp;h=720&amp;q=80" width="1280"/></figure>
<h3>A Thanksgiving Storm Pushes the Macy's Parade to Saturday</h3>
<p><a href="https://apnews.com/article/macys-thanksgiving-parade-postponed-weather-cdc870899c8dc2d32a0ac3d86119b3ba">Macy's postponed its 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday because of bad weather</a>, AP reports. It is the first postponement in the parade's history. <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-postponed/">The parade will now kick off Saturday at 8:30 a.m.</a>, CBS New York reports.</p><p><a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355">Mayor Zohran Mamdani said New Yorkers could see gusts as high as 65 mph on Thursday</a>, NBC News reports. Gov. Kathy Hochul declared a state of emergency for New York City and three nearby counties, CBS New York reports. NBC will air a rerun of the 2025 parade instead.</p></article>

<h3>A FedEx Cargo Jet Overruns a Runway at Miami International Airport</h3>
<p><a href="https://apnews.com/article/miami-airport-fedex-plane-off-runway-07567f757a4cef4356dfa4fc3bf5e561">A FedEx plane overran a runway at Miami International Airport on Wednesday night</a>, AP reports. All but one runway closed temporarily. There were no reports of injuries, the airport said. By early Thursday, the FAA reported hourslong flight delays.</p><p><a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/fedex-airplane-overruns-runway-miami-airport-temporarily-closing-1-run-rcna611417">The FAA said the McDonnell Douglas MD-11 was coming from Memphis and that it is investigating</a>, NBC News reports. FedEx said the plane had "an issue upon landing." FedEx resumed flying MD-11s in May after a UPS MD-11 crash in Kentucky last year.</p></article>

<h3>Global Stocks Rebound While Wall Street Takes Thanksgiving Off</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/stock-markets-today-.html">Europe's Stoxx 600 was up 1.6% as of 4:24 a.m. ET Thursday</a>, CNBC reports. Japan's Nikkei 225 rose 0.86%. U.S. markets are closed Thursday and will have a shortened session Friday. On Wednesday, the S&amp;P 500 closed down 0.25% and the Nasdaq fell 0.62%.</p><p>Chip stocks led the rebound after a bruising AI-driven sell-off, CNBC reports. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/asia-pacific/air-cargo-demand-ai-data-centres-shows-no-sign-slowing-sats-ceo-says-2026-11-26/">The head of air cargo handler SATS said AI data-centre demand shows no sign of slowing</a>, Reuters reports. <h3 id="dd-h">Why Kim Jong Un Is Showing Off His Nuclear Factories</h3><p><strong>What happened.</strong> <a href="https://apnews.com/article/north-korea-kim-nuclear-uranium-trump-78d6642ea055e8257518c3d695a58cd4">Kim Jong Un visited facilities that produce nuclear ingredients and called for building more nuclear weapons</a>, AP reports. Some images were blurred.</p><p><strong>Why it matters.</strong> <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/world/asia/north-korea-kim-jong-un-presses-wider-nuclear-deployment-rcna611419">Kim said North Korea should focus on using tactical nuclear weapons across multiple domains</a>, Reuters reports via NBC News. One analyst said it may be the first look at a naval reactor program.</p><p><strong>What each side says.</strong> Kim said North Korea's status as a nuclear weapons state is "absolutely inviolable," KCNA reported. <a href="https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/kim-jong-un-says-north-korea-should-widen-deployment-of-tactical-nukes">He warned that attempts to deny that status would be seen as "an open declaration of war"</a>, Al Jazeera reports. Kim accused the U.S. of urging allies to possess nuclear-powered submarines, AP reports. Experts say he wants to show off his arsenal, AP reports.</p><p><strong>What comes next.</strong> The tour came days after Kim ignored President Trump's offer to meet during a recent Asia visit, AP reports. Experts said Kim wants to reaffirm his rejection of talks on dismantling his program. <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/world/asia/north-korea-kim-jong-un-presses-wider-nuclear-deployment-rcna611419">Questions persist over prospects for renewed dialogue with the U.S.</a>, Reuters reports.
