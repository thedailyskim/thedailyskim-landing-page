Measles Cases Pass 5,000 Across Most of the U.S.

The United States has recorded 5,182 confirmed measles cases so far this year, according to the latest CDC data. Al Jazeera reports that this is the highest annual count since 1991. Cases have been confirmed in 45 states and Washington, D.C., ABC News reports.

About 96% of cases involve people who were unvaccinated or whose vaccination status is unknown, according to ABC’s review of CDC figures. Al Jazeera reports a dispute over classifying measles deaths, leaving federal and state totals different. The latest data also show lower kindergarten vaccination coverage than before the pandemic.

Technology Shares Rebound Without Erasing the Week’s Losses

Technology stocks regained some ground Friday after a sharp selloff, but Wall Street still ended the week lower. Reuters reports that the Nasdaq gained about 1.1% while the Dow fell 0.46%. Chipmakers led the rebound, following worries about AI infrastructure spending and possible tighter regulation.

The recovery was uneven across regions and indexes. Reuters reports that the Nasdaq posted a fourth weekly decline. Investors also weighed stronger U.S. hiring data and the outlook for interest rates. A positive session for some technology shares did not resolve concerns about the cost of the AI buildout.

New U.S. Strikes Come as Qatar Keeps Iran Mediation Active

Trump said U.S. forces carried out limited strikes against an Iranian air defense system Friday. AP reports that Iranian state media described explosions on Qeshm Island, near the Strait of Hormuz. Trump described the action as minor but did not provide detailed information about the target.

Diplomacy continued alongside the military action. Al Jazeera reports that Qatar rejected claims that negotiations had failed on Saturday. Qatar’s prime minister said mediation remained active to prevent further escalation. Neither those statements nor Trump’s claims of progress establish that the parties have reached an agreement.

AST Buys Engine Capacity While Keeping Other Launch Providers

AST SpaceMobile is moving into rocket development with a Firefly engine agreement worth more than $1.3 billion. Reuters reports that Blacklift would support AST’s satellite broadband network. The planned vehicle could carry five BlueBird satellites, with a debut targeted for late 2028 rather than an announced launch date.

The purchase is not a complete break with existing launch companies. Reuters describes the deal as a bid for more reliable access to space. AST says it still has more than 100 outside launch agreements. Firefly will supply engines initially, while licensing lets AST eventually manufacture them itself.

What Miller’s Promotion Changes Inside the White House

What happened. Trump selected Stephen Miller to succeed Susie Wiles as chief of staff, with the change taking effect Monday. ABC reports that Wiles will move to Trump’s political operation. Miller currently serves as deputy chief of staff for policy. His promotion follows Republican losses that will put Democrats in control of Congress.

Why it matters. The chief of staff coordinates the president’s team and helps shape access and priorities. Miller also brings extensive policy experience. AP describes his knowledge of executive power and federal government. His appointment puts an architect of Trump’s immigration program at the center of the administration as congressional scrutiny is expected to increase.

What each side says. Trump praised Wiles, who said her commitment to his agenda would continue. Democratic Rep. Robert Garcia criticized Miller’s appointment as doubling down on policies voters had rejected. AP reports those contrasting reactions after Tuesday’s Republican defeat. Miller spent the campaign defending Trump’s policies, while Wiles helped run his successful 2024 campaign.

What comes next. Miller begins Monday, as Democrats prepare to control both chambers of Congress next year. AP reports that Garcia is set to chair the House Oversight Committee. Miller’s background includes service as a homeland security adviser and coordination across federal agencies. Those roles add to his experience implementing immigration policy before this move into staff leadership.
