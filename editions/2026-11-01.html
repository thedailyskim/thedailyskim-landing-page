Google Lost Nearly $500 Billion in a Day. Hackers Stole $6.3 Billion in Crypto. And Ukraine Just Got the OK for 500 Patriots.

Wall Street's love affair with AI turned into a stress test this week. Alphabet and Intel took historic hits, while hackers linked to North Korea pulled off what looks like the largest crypto theft ever.

01

The Headlines

$6.3 Billion Vanished From Binance in About 20 Minutes

Binance, the world's largest crypto exchange, said on Friday, Oct. 30 that more than $6 billion was drained from its online "hot" wallets. Blockchain firms including TRM Labs, Chainalysis and Elliptic tied the attack to TraderTraitor, a North Korean hacking unit, and TRM said most of the money moved in roughly 20 minutes. Binance traced the breach to an engineer's compromised laptop, froze about $3 billion with industry partners and promised to cover customer losses. The previous record was the roughly $1.5 billion Bybit theft in 2025.

Sources: AP via PBS NewsHour · TechCrunch

Google's Parent Just Had the Worst Day in Its Stock-Market History

Alphabet's Class A shares fell 12.6% on Thursday, Oct. 29, the steepest drop in more than two decades of trading, erasing close to $500 billion in value. The trigger was its earnings report: search ad growth slowed to 12%, the weakest in five quarters, while quarterly capital spending topped $51 billion and pushed free cash flow negative. Alphabet now expects to spend up to $205 billion this year, and investors worry its own AI search tools are eating into the ads that pay for it all.

Sources: Fortune · TechCrunch

Ukraine Gets the Green Light to Buy 500 Patriot Interceptors

The State Department on Oct. 30 approved a possible $2.1 billion sale of 500 PAC-3 MSE missiles to Ukraine, the most advanced Patriot interceptor and one of the few weapons able to stop ballistic missiles. Ukraine will pay with funds from Canada, Germany and Norway plus an EU loan rather than U.S. money. No delivery date was given, and President Zelensky warned it could take years. Ukraine's supply of these interceptors ran out earlier in October.

Sources: CNN via KEYT

The Context

Is the AI Spending Boom Starting to Crack?

What happened

On Friday, Oct. 30, the S&P 500 fell 1.8%, its worst day since June, and the Nasdaq dropped 2.9%. Intel sank 28.6%, its worst day since 1974, after a weak forecast. Nvidia, Broadcom and Qualcomm slid too, and Amazon fell after reporting $42.4 billion in negative free cash flow over the past year.

Why it matters

Alphabet, Amazon, Meta and Microsoft plan to spend more than $740 billion this year, much of it on AI data centers, and big tech borrowing has roughly tripled to $326 billion. Demand for AI computing is still strong. What changed is patience: investors now want proof the spending will actually pay off.

What to watch

Whether next year's spending plans keep climbing, whether free cash flow turns positive again, how much more debt the tech giants take on, and whether AI chat search keeps slowing Google's ad growth.

Sources: AP via NBC Bay Area · TechCrunch
