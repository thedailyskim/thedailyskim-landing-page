The Headlines

Wall Street Just Had Its Worst Day Since June, and Intel Its Worst Since 1974

Worries that the AI spending boom won't pay off hit stocks hard on Friday, Oct. 30. The S&P 500 fell 1.8%, its worst day since June, and the Nasdaq dropped 2.9%. Intel sank 28.6% after a weak forecast, its worst day since 1974. Nvidia, Broadcom and Qualcomm slid too, and Amazon fell after reporting $42.4 billion in negative free cash flow over the past 12 months. It capped a third straight losing week for the S&P 500.

Sources: AP via NBC Bay Area · TechCrunch

A Judge Just Ended Trump's Lawsuit Against The Wall Street Journal. It Can't Be Refiled

A federal judge in Miami on Saturday, Oct. 31 permanently dismissed President Trump's defamation suit against The Wall Street Journal and Rupert Murdoch. The case, which AP says sought $10 billion, centered on the Journal's report about a letter said to bear Trump's name in a 2003 birthday album for Jeffrey Epstein. Trump denies writing it. Judge Darrin Gayles ruled the complaint did not show "actual malice," the bar public figures must clear. A Trump spokesperson said he will press forward, and an appeal is expected.

Sources: AP via PBS NewsHour · CBS News

Ukraine Gets the Green Light to Buy 500 Patriot Interceptors

The State Department on Oct. 30 approved a possible $2.1 billion sale of 500 PAC-3 MSE missiles to Ukraine, the most advanced Patriot interceptor and one of the few weapons able to stop ballistic missiles. Ukraine will pay with funds from Canada, Germany and Norway plus an EU loan rather than U.S. money. No delivery date was given, and President Zelensky warned it could take years. Ukraine's supply of these interceptors ran out earlier in October.

Sources: CNN via KEYT

Courts Just Blocked the Justice Department's Push to Set Aside 20,000 California Ballots

A federal appeals panel voted 2-1 on Friday, Oct. 30, to reject a Justice Department request that California set aside mail ballots sent to about 20,000 people the department says may not be citizens. State officials call that list highly unreliable. The majority said there was no workable way to pull the ballots without "potentially catastrophic effects" on the election. Later that day, U.S. District Judge Trina L. Thompson barred the department from threatening or coercing election workers. The DOJ says it will appeal to the Supreme Court.

Sources: AP via KSAT

Milwaukee Just Threw Its First-Ever World Series Parade

Brewers fans packed downtown Milwaukee on Friday, Oct. 30, for the franchise's first World Series championship parade, with many arriving hours early. The Brewers clinched the title two days earlier with a 4-1 win over the Tampa Bay Rays, taking the series in five games. The parade rolled down Wisconsin Avenue, much like the route used for the 1982 team that lost the Series in seven games. Milwaukee Public Schools canceled classes for the day.

Sources: AP via 6abc
