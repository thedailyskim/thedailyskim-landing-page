Trump Orders a Review of Democrats Without Providing Evidence of a Crime

Trump said Saturday that he was directing the Justice Department to examine Democrats over alleged threats against Republicans. Reuters reports that he supplied no evidence for calling their actions criminal. Democrats have promised congressional hearings and investigations after winning control of both chambers, with the new Congress taking office in January.

The president also singled out Sen. Elissa Slotkin, referring to her comments about accountability for administration officials. CNBC reports that Trump did not identify the alleged communications, senders or recipients. An earlier Justice Department inquiry examined Slotkin and other lawmakers over a video urging refusal of illegal orders. A grand jury declined to indict them.

Israel’s Coalition Search Moves to Eisenkot, With No Majority Assured

Gadi Eisenkot has the first opportunity to assemble Israel’s next government after President Isaac Herzog selected him Saturday night. AP reports that neither his bloc nor Netanyahu’s won a majority. The former military chief faces negotiations with parties that disagree over prospective partners and the direction of a future government.

The first deadline gives him 28 days, with a possible extension of 14. ABC reports that Eisenkot will receive the formal offer Sunday. Support in consultations is not the same as a coalition deal. Netanyahu remains a potential negotiating partner, while Arab led parties could influence whether another arrangement can win parliamentary support.

Flooded Gaza Tents Expose Gaps in Shelter and Storm Response

Families in Gaza tried to rescue bedding and reinforce tents after heavy rain flooded displacement camps Saturday. Reuters reports that civil defense officials received 1,500 distress calls. Local officials said shortages of fuel and working equipment limited their response. Many residents remain in temporary shelters after destruction of homes during the war.

Israel’s aid agency said it had stepped up sanitation preparations ahead of the storm. Reuters reports that COGAT described four additional vacuum trucks arriving that week. Hamas accused Israel of restricting shelter materials; the agency did not immediately address that allegation. Residents told Reuters they lacked tarpaulins and tents, while bedding was soaked.

Japan Assesses Damage After an Overnight Earthquake in Shizuoka

An earthquake shook central Japan early Sunday, breaking store windows and injuring at least 22 people in reporting checked at 10:49 UTC. AP reports that Japan’s Meteorological Agency measured the quake at magnitude 5.7. It struck at 1:04 a.m. local time, with its center in Shizuoka Prefecture.

The agency warned that further strong quakes could follow over the coming week. AP reports shattered windows and scattered books in affected communities. The U.S. Geological Survey measured the quake at 5.5, a separate agency estimate. Bullet trains were running at reduced speed as a precaution while authorities assessed the effects.

Deep dive

What Poland’s Investigators Are Examining After the Krakow Shooting

What happened. A gunman opened fire in Krakow’s Main Square Saturday, killing six people before police shot and detained him. AP reports that national mourning began Sunday and the square remained partly closed. Police also found three bodies at the suspect’s family home elsewhere in Poland. The investigation covers both locations.

Why it matters. The attack struck a busy tourist destination in a country where mass shootings are uncommon. Reuters reports that police and national security agencies are investigating. Officers who confronted the suspect were wounded and remain hospitalized. Authorities praised their intervention, saying it prevented further deaths as people fled the square.

What each side says. Interior Minister Marcin Kierwinski said evidence pointed to an influence from extreme nationalist ideology. AP reports his description of the suspect’s radical views. These are officials’ assessments during an active investigation, not a completed judicial finding. Police confirmed the suspect’s age as 24 after conflicting initial reports.

What comes next. Investigators must establish the sequence of events, the weapons used and the relationship between the two crime scenes. Reuters reports that officers found weapons and ammunition in the suspect’s vehicle. AP reports that police identified the bodies at his home as his parents and sister. National mourning continues Monday.
