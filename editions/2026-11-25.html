TSA Expects 17.6 Million Flyers as the Holiday Rush Begins

TSA said it expects to screen about 17.6 million air travelers from Tuesday, Nov. 24, through Monday, Nov. 30. The agency projects more than 3 million passengers on Sunday, Nov. 29, the busiest day of the period. It urges arriving two hours early for domestic flights.

NOAA forecast scattered showers and a few thunderstorms in the Southeast on the busy pre-Thanksgiving travel day. Later Wednesday, wet snow and rain are possible in the mid-Mississippi Valley. Flood watches cover Florida's east coast, including Miami, through Thursday, NBC News reports.

Trump Gives Rudy Giuliani the Presidential Medal of Freedom

President Trump presented Rudy Giuliani with the Presidential Medal of Freedom on Tuesday, CBS News reports. The White House praised his work as a federal prosecutor and as New York City mayor. Giuliani said he was "honored and humbled."

Giuliani represented Trump in efforts to overturn the 2020 election results, NBC News reports. That work led to his disbarment in New York and Washington, D.C. Cases against him in Georgia and Arizona were dropped. He has denied the allegations.

Anthropic Falls Below Its IPO Price as Tech Stocks Slide

Anthropic shares fell below their IPO price after a 13% sell-off Tuesday, CNBC reports. Trading under $76, the stock was 8.5% below its offering level. It took just four days, compared with 21 trading days for SpaceX.

The Nasdaq and the S&P 500 closed lower Tuesday as AI shares gave back gains, Reuters reports via NBC News. As of early Wednesday, S&P 500 futures were down 1.4%, CNBC reports. Traders were also looking ahead to fresh October inflation data, CNBC said.

Sanofi Agrees to Buy Insmed for $41 Billion

Sanofi said Tuesday it agreed to acquire U.S. drugmaker Insmed for $175 per share in cash. The company put the total at $41.0 billion, or EUR 37.6 billion. The deal adds two commercialized respiratory medicines.

If closed, it would be the biggest pharma deal since Pfizer bought Seagen in December 2023, CNBC reports. Insmed shares closed up 14.1% Tuesday, CNBC reports. Sanofi said it expects the deal to close in the first half of 2027.

Why Bangladesh Garment Workers Won a 36% Raise

What happened. Bangladesh raised the minimum monthly wage for entry-level garment workers by 36% on Wednesday, AP reports. The new floor is 17,000 takas, about $136, up from 12,500 takas. It came after weeks of violent protests that closed factories and disrupted production. Workers had demanded the minimum wage be doubled.

Why it matters. Garments make up about 80% of Bangladesh's exports, mostly to the United States and Europe, AP reports. The sector directly employs about 4 million workers, mostly women from rural areas. Bangladesh is the world's second-largest garment exporter, behind China, Al Jazeera reports, which shapes clothing supply chains.

What each side says. Protesters had asked for 30,000 takas, Al Jazeera reports. A labor representative on the wage board called the outcome "not entirely bad" and urged workers back to factories. A manufacturers group said the protests cost the industry an estimated $428 million in losses. It said $254 million in orders moved to other countries.

What comes next. The wage hike will take effect in December, with new salaries paid in January, Al Jazeera reports. The six-member board approved the wage in a 4-2 vote, AP reports. It was not immediately clear if workers would accept the new structure after weeks of protests, AP reports.
