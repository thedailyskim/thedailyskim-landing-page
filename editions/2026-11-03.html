The Headlines

Congress Is Up for Grabs Today, and So Are 145 Ballot Measures

All 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats and 36 governor's races are on today's ballot. Republicans currently hold a 53-47 Senate majority and a narrow edge in the House. More than 57 million people voted early. Voters will also decide 145 ballot measures, including abortion questions in Missouri, Idaho, Nevada and Virginia, voter ID proposals in several states, and redistricting questions in Missouri, Maryland and Colorado.

Sources: CBS News · AP via Live 5 News

Obamacare Sign-Ups Just Opened, and Premiums Are Expected to Jump 13%

Open enrollment for Affordable Care Act marketplace plans is underway in most states and runs through Jan. 15. A KFF analysis of new federal data, reported by AP, projects average marketplace premiums will rise 13% for 2027, driven mostly by rising health care costs and hospital consolidation. Subsidies are now narrower for some groups, which KFF says could leave about 1 million more people uninsured over time. About 19.2 million people were enrolled as of February.

Sources: AP via PBS NewsHour

Netflix Now Wants to Pick Your Next Show Based on Your Mood

Netflix began rolling out "AI Discover" on Tuesday, a new tab that suggests titles based on your mood, how much time you have, or what's trending. Viewers pick from themes like "something light," "scare me" or "family movie night," then refine or start over. A new button lets users save a title or hide it from future picks. The test starts with English-language viewers in the U.S. on supported TVs and expands in the coming weeks.

Sources: TechCrunch

CrowdStrike Is Spending Nearly $500 Million to Guard Against Rogue AI

Cybersecurity company CrowdStrike is buying two AI security startups for about $495 million combined, according to The Wall Street Journal. Fiddler AI, roughly $400 million, makes software that monitors AI agents inside companies. Dreadnode, about $95 million, automates testing to find flaws in AI models and apps before they launch. The deals show how fast businesses are adopting AI, often faster than their security policies can keep up.

Sources: TechCrunch

Apple Will Make Dating Apps and AI Companions 18+ Only

Apple told developers on Monday that dating apps and apps with romantic features, including AI companion apps, must carry an 18+ rating in the App Store, even when those features are just one part of the app. Apps that share a user's real-world location with others must also disclose it. Starting Nov. 16, developers must add new content descriptors so age ratings match each app's features.

Sources: TechCrunch
