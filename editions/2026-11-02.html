The big read

Peace talk hopes knocked nearly $9 off a barrel of oil

Brent crude settled down 7.6% at $104.77 a barrel on Monday, a drop of nearly $9. U.S. crude fell 7.4% to $96.46.

The selloff followed talks between Iranian and Qatari officials on ending the war that began in late February. Analysts urged caution after months of false starts, noting attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz remain a risk.

A sitting U.S. senator had a stroke at his son's hockey game

Connecticut Sen. Chris Murphy, 53, is in the hospital after a stroke, his office said Sunday. Symptoms began Saturday at his son's hockey game, and he was moved to Johns Hopkins for monitoring.

His chief of staff said he is awake, alert and responding well, and his office expects a full recovery. Senators from both parties sent well wishes. Murphy is not on the ballot this week.

Investigators say a jet may have been breaking apart before it crashed in Florida

A Learjet 60 flying from Austin to the Bahamas crashed in Greenacres, Palm Beach County, on Sunday, killing all nine people aboard.

NTSB member Tom Chapman said there are signs the jet was breaking up before it hit the ground. The crew had reported ice and turbulence at 35,000 feet. No cause has been determined, and about 77 nearby mobile homes were evacuated.

ChatGPT is merging its Chat and Work tabs into one

OpenAI told users it is combining the Chat and Work tabs in the ChatGPT desktop app. People can go from brainstorming an idea to having ChatGPT carry it out in one place.

The change goes to select users first before a wider rollout. ChatGPT and Codex will now share one usage allowance, which OpenAI says won't affect most users.

Deep dive

El Niño storms flood the desert Southwest as rivers hit record levels

What happened. A Pacific storm system fueled by El Niño soaked the desert Southwest from Oct. 30 into Nov. 1. Forecasters warned of up to 4 inches of rain on already saturated ground. Phoenix firefighters rescued people from four flooded vehicles, and Henderson, Nevada, made three swift water rescues.

Why it matters. The region was already waterlogged after flooding the week before killed 2 people in New Mexico. Some rivers reached their highest levels in 50 to 100 years, and parts of Sedona were urged to evacuate. Forecasters say a Super El Niño could be declared later this year, pointing to a wet, dangerous winter.

What each side says. Allison Santorelli of the Weather Prediction Center said, "It's not going to take much rainfall to be impactful at all in some of these locations." Scottsdale Fire Capt. Dave Folio warned that just 2 inches of water can sweep a vehicle away. In Pagosa Springs, Colorado, residents were ordered to evacuate and avoid the San Juan River.

What comes next. The storm was set to reach the Plains and Midwest on Nov. 2, with nearly 40 million people facing a flash flood threat from Texas to Wisconsin. Severe storms and a few tornadoes were possible in Kansas, Oklahoma and northern Texas. The system could bring rain, storms or snow to parts of 35 states this week.
