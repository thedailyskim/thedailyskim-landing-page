The Headlines

Peace-Talk Hopes Just Knocked Nearly $9 Off a Barrel of Oil

Brent crude settled down 7.6% at $104.77 a barrel on Monday, a drop of nearly $9, and U.S. crude fell 7.4% to $96.46. The selloff followed talks between Iranian and Qatari officials on ending the war that began in late February, after Iran's president said the supreme leader had authorized negotiations. Analysts urged caution after months of false starts, noting that attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz remain a risk.

Sources: Reuters via Investing.com · Reuters via Yahoo Finance

A Sitting U.S. Senator Had a Stroke at His Son's Hockey Game

Connecticut Sen. Chris Murphy, 53, is in the hospital after a stroke, his office said Sunday. Symptoms began Saturday at his son's hockey game, and he was moved to Johns Hopkins for monitoring. His chief of staff said he is awake, alert and responding well, and his office expects a full recovery. Senators from both parties sent well wishes. Murphy is not on the ballot this week.

Sources: AP via PBS NewsHour

A "Super" El Niño Just Flooded the Desert Southwest

Pacific storms dumped up to 4 inches of rain across the Southwest over the weekend, the National Weather Service said, prompting evacuations in parts of Arizona, Colorado and New Mexico, including Sedona, Durango and Pagosa Springs. Flood warnings or watches covered nearly all of Arizona. Scientists told AP an unusually strong El Niño is loading the air with extra moisture and making heavy rain more likely, though it isn't the only cause. They expect El Niño to peak in December.

Sources: AP via PBS NewsHour · AP via PBS NewsHour

Investigators Say a Jet May Have Been Breaking Apart Before It Crashed in Florida

A Learjet 60 flying from Austin to the Bahamas crashed in Greenacres, in Palm Beach County, on Sunday, killing all nine people aboard. NTSB member Tom Chapman said there are signs the jet was breaking up before it hit the ground, and the crew had reported ice and turbulence at 35,000 feet. No cause has been determined. About 77 mobile homes near the crash site were evacuated.

Sources: AP via PBS NewsHour · ABC News

ChatGPT Is Merging Its Chat and Work Tabs Into One

OpenAI told users it is combining the Chat and Work tabs in the ChatGPT desktop app, so people can go from brainstorming an idea to having ChatGPT carry it out in one place, like building a presentation or doing tasks on the web. The change goes to select users first for feedback before a wider rollout. ChatGPT and Codex will now share one usage allowance, which OpenAI says won't affect most users.

Sources: TechCrunch
