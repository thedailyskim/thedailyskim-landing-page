Stocks Post Their Best Day Since April 2025 as Huang Returns to Nvidia

The S&P 500 rose 4.73% on Monday to close at 7,237.17, CNBC reports. The Nasdaq Composite jumped 6.51%, and the Dow gained 1,447.98 points. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq posted their best days since April 2025. Nvidia soared 31% after its board returned Jensen Huang as CEO, CNBC reports.

Nvidia said its board received input from senior levels of the U.S. government, CBS News reports. Huang resigned less than two weeks earlier amid a scandal over an affair with a woman U.S. intelligence agencies suspected of spying for China. China has denied it. Trump said he convinced the board to reinstate Huang, ABC News reports.

Rep. Jimmy Gomez Says He Will Resign After a House Ethics Report

Rep. Jimmy Gomez said Monday he will resign from Congress, CBS News reports. The House Ethics Committee said it found "substantial evidence" the California Democrat engaged in sexual misconduct with a House staffer. The committee said the behavior "could constitute criminal misdemeanor sexual abuse."

Gomez said he had "strong disagreements" with the findings but saw "no other alternative than to resign," CBS News reports. Gomez, who lost his reelection bid in November, said he will resign on Dec. 1, ABC News reports. He said he takes full responsibility for personal mistakes outside his marriage.

An Appeals Court Gives Trump a New Shot at Moving His Hush Money Case

An appeals court on Monday opened the door to moving Trump's New York criminal case to federal court, NBC News reports. A 2nd Circuit panel said a lower court judge "erred" in ruling Trump waited too long to raise an immunity defense. It directed a reconsideration of his motion.

In federal court, Trump could try to overturn his conviction on presidential immunity grounds, ABC News reports. A Manhattan jury convicted him on 34 felony counts of falsifying business records. Trump has denied any wrongdoing, NBC News reports. A spokesperson for his legal team praised the "prompt and correct ruling."

The U.S. Targets Iran's Shadow Banking Networks With New Sanctions

The Treasury Department said Monday it sanctioned over two dozen Iranian exchange houses, front companies and key personnel. It said the goal is to disrupt Iran's global shadow banking networks. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called the system "the last financial lifeline of a bankrupt regime."

The move came shortly after Trump threatened renewed military action next year, Al Jazeera reports. The Treasury claimed the entities help Iran move billions of dollars despite comprehensive sanctions. The Central Bank of Iran oversees the designated entities, according to the Treasury statement.

Why Two States Are Suing SpaceX Over Grok Images

What happened. Virginia and Oregon became the first states to sue SpaceX over its Grok AI image generator, CNBC reports. They allege SpaceX harmed consumers and falsely marketed Grok as generally safe. SpaceX is the parent company of X, according to the Virginia attorney general. Oregon filed its complaint Sunday evening, and Virginia filed Monday morning, CNBC reports.

Why it matters. Research shows Grok let users make explicit deepfakes from real images of children and women without consent, CNBC reports. A study cited by Virginia estimated Grok posted about three million sexualized images on X between Dec. 29, 2025, and Jan. 8, 2026. About 23,000 appeared to depict children, the study estimated.

What each side says. Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones said "false reassurances" from the company misled users. Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield said a lack of safety measures was "considered a feature, not a bug" there. SpaceX said it acts on accounts and notifies law enforcement when people try to break its safeguards, CNBC reports.

What comes next. Both states are seeking injunctions, civil penalties, disgorgement and legal costs, CNBC reports. Virginia also wants restitution for consumers. As of early Monday, the suits were pending the courts' acceptance, CNBC reported. Jones called the filings the first step toward holding the company accountable, his office said.
