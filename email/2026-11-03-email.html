The Headlines

Congress Is Up for Grabs Today, and So Are 145 Ballot Measures

All 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats and 36 governor's races are on today's ballot. Republicans currently hold a 53-47 Senate majority and a narrow edge in the House. More than 57 million people voted early. Voters will also decide 145 ballot measures, including abortion questions in Missouri, Idaho, Nevada and Virginia, voter ID proposals in several states, and redistricting questions in Missouri, Maryland and Colorado.

Sources: CBS News · AP via Live 5 News

Photo: Michael Afonso / Unsplash

The U.S. Struck Iranian Forces on the Eve of the Vote

A U.S. official told AP that on Monday the U.S. carried out limited strikes on Iranian forces in the Strait of Hormuz, describing them as defensive after those forces fired surface-to-air missiles at U.S. assets. Iranian state TV reported explosions on Qeshm Island. They are the first known U.S. strikes since early September in a war now more than eight months old. Qatar is mediating talks on a framework in which Iran would halt attacks in the strait and the U.S. would lift its naval blockade.

Sources: AP via PBS NewsHour

Photo: Mika Baumeister / Unsplash

A Child Died of Measles in New York, the First Such Death in 30+ Years

New York health officials said Monday that a child in Lewis County, in the state's north, died in connection with the ongoing measles outbreak. The child's age and vaccination status were not released. Gov. Kathy Hochul declared a state disaster emergency over the outbreak about a month ago. Nationally, more than 4,800 cases have been confirmed this year, the most since 1991, and at least 96% were in people who were unvaccinated or whose status was unknown.

Sources: ABC News

The Context

Photo: Louis Velazquez / Unsplash

What's at Stake Tonight, and Why Results May Take Days

What happened

Americans are choosing every House member, a third of the Senate and 36 governors. Republicans hold the Senate 53-47, and the House is closely divided. CBS counts 47 battleground House districts and 11 key Senate races, including Texas, Maine, North Carolina, Georgia and Michigan.

Why it matters

Whichever party controls each chamber sets the agenda and oversight for the next two years. Ballot measures can directly change state law on abortion, voter ID and how district maps are drawn, and nine states are voting under new congressional maps.

What to watch

AP will tally nearly 7,000 races and calls a race only when trailing candidates have no path left to win. With so many ballots cast early or by mail, close races may not be called tonight.

Sources: CBS News · AP via PBS NewsHour
